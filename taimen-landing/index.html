How it works

From your data to a model you own

01

Data

We sign a contract, review your data in an isolated environment, agree on a quality metric and fix the price.

02

Training

Runs on our own hardware. We show intermediate checkpoints — not "please wait for the result".

03

Metric

We validate the agreed metric on a held-out slice of your data — you accept the work by a number, not by a feeling.

04

Handover

You get the weights, inference code and a deployment guide. The model stays yours.
