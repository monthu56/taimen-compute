How it works

Every answer is a receipt, not a guess

A typed knowledge graph plus vector search over your company's memory — people, decisions, documents and how they connect. Every answer carries the original source; knowledge bases stay isolated by namespace.

01

Retain

Paste an article as-is: it is chunked, linked into the graph and indexed. The original is kept verbatim.

02

Recall

Ask in natural language. Hybrid search — vector, full-text, graph neighbours — returns the most relevant passages with a score.

03

Verify

One click opens the full original with its provenance: where it came from, when it was last seen, with what confidence.

What it's for

Memory is the foundation — a human decides

Your personal assistant

Ask about a policy, a past decision, or who owns a project — you get the answer with its exact source and the people behind it. You read it and decide.

Agents on the same memory

Process agents work alongside your team with their own roles and access: a tendering agent knows every tender and drafts the strongest bid. You review and sign.

Experience that stays

Every decision, document and expert is retained and searchable. When someone leaves, their experience stays — and a new hire gets sourced answers from day one.

Pilot

Run this on your own knowledge base

Point the engine at your handbook, policies or docs and get verifiable answers with the source attached. Your data never leaves your isolated instance.
