What it is

Not flat RAG — memory of people and decisions

GRAPH

A typed knowledge graph

Documents link to people, decisions, projects and concepts. Retrieval follows the company graph, not just text similarity.

VERIFY

Answers with a source

Every passage carries a score and provenance: where it came from, when it was seen. One click opens the stored original. Nothing is invented.

ISOLATED

An isolated instance

One customer — one instance with its own database. Knowledge bases are separated by namespace, access is key-gated. Your data is never used for other projects.

How it works

From documents to verifiable answers

01

Retain

Your documents — handbook, policies, meeting notes — are chunked, linked into the graph and indexed. Originals are kept verbatim.

02

Recall

Ask in natural language. Hybrid search — vector, full-text, graph neighbours — returns the most relevant passages with a score.

03

Verify

Every answer opens down to the original with its provenance. You decide on the document, not on a model's paraphrase.

04

Integrate

HTTP API, an MCP server and a CLI: the same memory is available to your assistants and process agents — with roles and scoped access.
